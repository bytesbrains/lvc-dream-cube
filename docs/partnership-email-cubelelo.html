Making Cubing Universal: Bringing the LVC Dream Cube to Life with Cubelelo

Dear Jainendra, Nitesh & the Cubelelo Leadership Team,

Cubelelo has pioneered the speedcubing movement in India. Your engineering with the Drift Explorer 3×3 series has established high quality, domestic puzzle production at an accessible price point across the country.

At Little Voice Club, we are taking that same passion into universal accessibility with the LVC Dream Cube: a tactile puzzle cube where every colour face features a unique embossed geometric pattern. It allows anyone to explore and solve the puzzle by touch—without altering the iconic speedcube mechanism.

“Same Play. More Possibilities. For Everyone.” — Solving by tactile recognition & spatial memory.

👁️ Blind & Visually Impaired Players: Complete tactile independence—solving entirely through touch without reliance on braille reading.

🎨 Colour-Vision Differences: Eliminates red/green/orange ambiguity with distinct physical textures.

🌙 Mainstream Speedcubers: A brand-new tactile category: speedcubing in the dark.

Why a Joint Partnership with Cubelelo?

Rather than treating accessibility as a niche side-project, our vision is to partner with India's premier cubing ecosystem to co-create an official edition: Cubelelo Drift × LVC Dream Cube.

1. Co-Branded Inclusive Line
Establish India’s first mass-produced tactile speedcube under a joint co-brand, distributed across Cubelelo.com, Amazon, and educational institutions worldwide.
2. Domestic Tooling & Moulding
Combine Kreativity Labs’ precision injection moulding in Raipur with LVC Dream’s tactile specs for seamless factory-fitted textured caps.
3. Global Cross-Promotion & Platform Presence
We will prominently highlight Cubelelo across the Little Voice Club platform, our live Kickstarter campaign, our open hardware repository, and global inclusive-education initiatives as our official manufacturing and distribution partner.
