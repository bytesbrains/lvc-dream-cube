Same toy, more ways to play

Close your eyes. It still works.

We 3D-printed small pattern chips and attached them to an ordinary puzzle cube. Suddenly the same toy can be experienced in new ways.

🌈

Beyond colour

A child with colour-vision differences can use texture alongside colour, so there's no more guessing red from green.

Play in the dark

The tactile key

Six colours, six textures.

Each texture is easy to tell apart from the others, from a smooth chip up to a tall, rounded dome. Every chip is 12 × 12 mm.

The working prototype

We finally held it in our hands.

Open source

Make your own, free.

We're giving the design away. Anyone with a 3D printer can make a Dream Cube today, whether that's a school, a maker space, a parent or a toy company.

Download full cube (.3mf) All files (.zip)

Released under CC BY 4.0. Print it, remix it, even sell it. Just credit LVC Dream / Little Voice Club.

  1. Get a cube

    Any ordinary 3×3 puzzle cube. The 12 mm chips fit a standard 57 mm cube.

  2. Print the chips

    One plate for a full cube, or six colour-matched plates. No supports needed.

  3. Glue & play

    Match each chip to its colour, let it cure, then close your eyes and play. Full guide
