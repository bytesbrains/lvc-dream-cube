# Contributing

LVC Dream is about making things everyone already loves work for more people. Contributions of every kind are welcome.

## Ways to help

- **Test it with real users.** Feedback from blind and visually impaired players, people with colour-vision differences, teachers and occupational therapists is the most valuable thing you can give us.
- **Improve the textures.** Suggest or model patterns that are easier to tell apart, kinder to small fingers, or quicker to print.
- **Add variants.** Other cube sizes (2×2, 4×4, mini cubes), braille-labelled chips, snap-fit or sticker-backed chips.
- **Improve the docs.** Better print settings for specific printers, translations, clearer assembly photos.

## How to contribute

1. Open an issue that describes the idea or the feedback.
2. For design changes, fork the repo and add your files under `models/`, named `<cube>_<count>x_<texture>_<colour>.3mf`. Include a photo of a real print if you can.
3. Open a pull request. By contributing you agree that your work is released under the repository's CC BY 4.0 licence.

## Partnerships

Toy makers, manufacturers, researchers and brands interested in producing or validating accessible products: email **hello@littlevoice.club**.
